Cookie policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy covers mh-sportsmassage.co.uk. Cookies and similar technologies can store information on your device. This site uses local storage to remember your privacy settings; local storage is not a cookie, but it is a similar browser-storage technology.

What this site uses

The website code does not include analytics, advertising pixels, embedded social feeds or an embedded booking service. Photographs and the treatment video are served from this website. Viewing the video does not contact a third-party video platform.

Local storage		Remembers whether you allow external Google Fonts. Stores a policy version number, your choice and a timestamp. Used only to apply your privacy choice.The choice is valid for 180 days. It is checked and expired records are removed when you visit the site. Your browser may retain an unused record until a later visit or until you clear site data.

Optional external fonts

You can choose Allow external fonts to load DM Sans and Outfit from Google Fonts. Requests are made to fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com; Google receives your IP address and technical request information. This site does not deliberately set an analytics or advertising cookie through these font requests.

Until you allow external fonts, the site uses fonts already available on your device and makes no Google Fonts requests. Choosing Essential only keeps external fonts disabled. You can browse the site and book without enabling them.

Changing your choice

Use in the footer or here to choose again. Selecting Essential only removes the Google Fonts stylesheet and prevents the site from loading it on future visits while that preference remains valid. Withdrawing your choice cannot undo requests already sent to Google. Your browser may retain previously downloaded files in its cache.

Both choices are remembered on this browser for up to 180 days. Clearing site data or using another browser may show the notice again. If browser storage is blocked, the site still works, but your choice may not be remembered after you leave.

External sites and hosting

Setmore booking, Facebook and Google Maps are external links. They are not embedded and are not loaded by this site's privacy banner. If you follow a link, that service's own privacy and cookie rules apply. Your choice here does not control cookies on those websites.

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information to serve and secure the website. See GitHub's privacy statement, Google's privacy policy and Setmore's privacy policy.
