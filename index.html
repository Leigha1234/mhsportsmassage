MASSAGE FAQs.
WE'VE GOT YOU.

New to massage? Here are a few things to know before your first appointment in Lossiemouth.

Do I need to play sport to book? +

Not at all. Sports massage can be suitable for people with everyday muscle tension as well as active individuals. Mitch can discuss your goals before treatment.

What happens at my first appointment? +

A client information form is emailed after booking. Complete it before your appointment so Mitch can review your needs and relevant health history. You'll discuss the treatment and agree on an approach you're comfortable with.

Will the massage be painful? +

Pressure is tailored to you. Let Mitch know if anything feels uncomfortable; treatment shouldn't require you to push through significant pain.

What should I wear? +

Comfortable clothing is a good starting point. Mitch can explain what access is needed for the treatment area while maintaining your privacy.

How much is a sports massage? +

A 50-minute sports massage costs £40. Multi-session packages and an eligible Elgin Swim Club member rate are also available. See the packages above or the booking page for options.

How do I book an appointment? +

Our new booking page is coming soon. For now, please use our Setmore booking page to book your appointment, or call 07368 911727.

Where is the clinic and where can I park? +

MH Sports Massage is based on Baker Street, Lossiemouth, IV31 6NX. Parking is available on Queen Street or Commerce Street. Please do not park under the Vallen Fitness sign.

What if I cannot find a suitable appointment? +

Contact Mitch to discuss extra availability. Mobile appointments are available on request, and additional costs may apply.
