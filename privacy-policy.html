Website privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This notice explains how MH Sports Massage handles information associated with this website and enquiries made using its contact details. It does not replace the privacy information needed for consultation forms, health information, treatment records or payment processing.

Who to contact

MH Sports Massage is responsible for its website and the enquiries it receives. Contact Mitch Haines at mhsportsmassage@outlook.com, call 07368 911727, or write to MH Sports Massage, Baker Street, Lossiemouth, IV31 6NX.

The site has no contact form, account registration, analytics or advertising tracking in its website code. There is no automated decision-making or profiling through this website.

Booking and health information

Booking currently takes place on Setmore, outside this website. Setmore handles information under its privacy notice, and MH Sports Massage receives information necessary to manage appointments.

Please ask Mitch for the relevant client privacy information before providing medical details or completing a consultation form. That information should explain the handling of health information, the applicable lawful basis and special-category condition, recipients and retention periods. Avoid sending sensitive medical details in a general website enquiry.

Services and sharing

  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website and processes technical requests to deliver and secure it.
  • Google Fonts: receives technical request information only if you choose to allow external fonts.
  • Setmore: provides the external booking service.
  • Email and telephone services: deliver enquiries sent using the contact details on this website.

Information may also be shared where required by law, or where necessary to obtain professional advice or resolve a dispute. We do not sell website enquiry information. The footer credit to The Organised Types and TOTS-OS is not an analytics integration; it does not itself transmit your browsing information to those businesses.

Read the privacy information published by GitHub, Google and Setmore. Facebook and Google Maps apply their own policies when you follow links to them.

International processing

Some external providers operate internationally and may process data outside the UK. Their notices describe locations and safeguards. Contact Mitch for information about safeguards applying to any international transfers made by MH Sports Massage in handling your enquiry.

How long information is kept

Your browser privacy choice remains valid for 180 days, as explained in the cookie policy. Enquiries are retained for as long as needed to answer and administer the enquiry, and then deleted unless needed for an appointment, legal obligation or dispute. Relevant factors include whether the enquiry is ongoing, whether you become a client and any applicable legal or insurance requirements. Ask Mitch about the period applying to your enquiry.

Technical hosting information is kept according to the hosting provider's own retention arrangements. Booking, consultation and treatment records require separate retention information from MH Sports Massage; this notice does not specify an unverified clinical-record period.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access to your personal information, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. These rights are subject to the conditions and exceptions in data protection law.

You can withdraw consent for external fonts using Cookie settings. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. To make a request about information held by MH Sports Massage, email mhsportsmassage@outlook.com.

Complaints

Contact Mitch if you have concerns. You can also complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Changes to this notice

This notice will be updated if the website's services or data use change. The date above identifies this version.
